A Tribute to 46 Golden Years

— Penned by Suman & Ravi

"Happy anniversary, dear friends, to forty six years of marriage,

"As you celebrate this milestone and the life that you have made,

May your marriage grow still stronger with every passing year,
And may our cherished friendship keep holding you both dear."
